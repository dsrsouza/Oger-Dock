# Community code of conduct

The Oger Dock community welcomes respectful technical discussion from users, developers and translators.

Be courteous, discuss ideas rather than attacking people, respect different experience levels, and avoid harassment, discriminatory abuse, spam or doxxing. Keep logs and screenshots free of private information.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation to protect constructive discussion. Report concerns through GitHub moderation or contact a maintainer privately when possible.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and other official project spaces.
